Inside the model

Python-based feature engineering feeds Monte Carlo race simulations, testing thousands of possible race scenarios before the results are presented in Power BI.

  1. 01OverviewPodium outlook, weather, safety-car context and fastest-lap likelihood.
  2. 02Detailed probabilitiesWin, podium, fastest-lap and DNF probabilities for every driver.
  3. 03Circuit analysisTrack-demand profile and driver suitability at each circuit.
  4. 04Predictions vs actual resultsCompare the model's predictions with race outcomes across three analysis modes.

About

I studied Computer Sciences and Business Management at ISCTE-IUL and BME as an Erasmus exchange student. I started my career at KPMG in Portugal as an IT Advisor, then moved to Italy to work at Kantar as a Data Service Consultant. I returned to Portugal because of the Covid outbreak, then moved to Austria to work at Global Blue as a Data Analyst. Boeing called soon after, so I moved to the Netherlands, where I first worked as a Business Intelligence Developer and then as a Data Product Owner. I have always worked with data. Different industries, different responsibilities, different geographies, but in the end data is always (almost) the same.
