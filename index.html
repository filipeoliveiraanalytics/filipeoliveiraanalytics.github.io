F1 2026 GP Predictor

This dashboard blends Python feature engineering and Monte Carlo race simulations into a Power BI product covering race-weekend forecasts, circuit analysis and prediction-vs-result tracking. Built as a living F1 forecasting model, updated as new sessions and results arrive.

  • Overview: podium outlook, weather and safety car context, and fastest lap likelihood.
  • Detailed probabilities: driver ranking table with win, podium, fastest lap and DNF probabilities.
  • Circuit analysis: track demand profile and driver suitability ranking by team colour.
  • Model inputs: track characteristics, recent form, driver priors, team pace assumptions, tyre behaviour, reliability signals, weather scenarios, safety car probability and race-weekend context.
  • Iteration loop: predictions can be refreshed before the weekend, after practice, after qualifying and after the race to compare model expectation against reality.
