Featured project F1 2026 Next GP Predictor

A brand new predictor engine built with Python (v5.4.3) and visualised in Power BI. It predicts Win %, DNF %, and final positions using Monte Carlo simulations.

  • Live Updates: Forecasts are rerun after Free Practices and Qualifying.
  • 3,000+ Monte Carlo race simulations factoring in Safety Cars, tyre degradation & driver style.
  • Fully integrated Python script automatically fetching session telemetry via FastF1.

F1 2026 Next GP Predictor

This dashboard blends Python-based feature engineering and Monte Carlo race simulations into a Power BI product focused on the next Grand Prix. It includes a premium F1-inspired interface and three pages designed for both storytelling and explainability.

  • Overview: podium outlook, weather and safety car context, and fastest lap likelihood.
  • Detailed probabilities: driver ranking table with win, podium, fastest lap and DNF probabilities.
  • Circuit analysis: track demand profile and driver suitability ranking by team colour.
  • Note: the model currently uses track characteristics, recent form, driver priors, team pace assumptions, tyre behaviour, reliability signals, weather scenarios, safety car probability among other factors. It is designed as a living project that can later support scenario-based controls directly from Python.
  • Live Updates: Predictions are dynamically updated after every Free Practice and Qualifying session.
